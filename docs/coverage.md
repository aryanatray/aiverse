## v0.2 learning experience

Implemented and verified in jsdom: a contextual guide for all eleven labs; real parameter interpretation; computation-flow diagrams; 33 concise term definitions; objectives and worked examples; detailed reading navigation; automatic playback; control-triggered updates/replay; manual pause and stepping; hidden-workspace and hidden-document suspension; timer cleanup; approval and terminal-failure boundaries; keyboard observation entry and validation; fresh retrieval on query changes.

Responsive three-column desktop, compact navigation, mobile/tablet guide banners, typography, and dark-theme plot colors are implemented in CSS but have **not** been inspected in an actual browser. They are not counted as a visual QA pass.

## Required domains with no dedicated functional lab yet

Agentic Frameworks (8), Computer Vision (9), MLOps/LLMOps/AgentOps (11), Data Engineering for AI (12), Responsible AI/Security (13), and Advanced AI Research (14) have taxonomy entries and starter-path reuse only. Statistics/probability and many other broad subtopics likewise remain planned even within domains that have a starter lab.

## Coverage rules

- **Planned**: entry exists in the taxonomy; not a lesson or feature claim.
- **Implemented starter**: actual bounded computation/simulation and written lesson exist.
- **Tested/passing**: only the concrete automated checks listed in the report passed.
- **Not verified in browser**: no screenshot, device behavior, FPS, accessibility audit, or end-to-end browser pass is claimed.
